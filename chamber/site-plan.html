WDD 231 Website Project Site Plan

Site Name

Benin City Chamber of Commerce

I chose this site name because it represents a real-world style organization that supports business growth in Benin City. It also matches the theme of my course project, which focuses on building a functional chamber of commerce website.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide information about the Benin City Chamber of Commerce. It will include business listings, local attractions, membership information, and key resources for businesses and visitors. The site is designed to promote economic activity and highlight important places within Benin City.

Scenarios

These are questions typical visitors or business owners might ask:

  • How can I join the Benin City Chamber of Commerce and what benefits do members receive?
  • What important business locations and attractions should I know about in Benin City?

Color Scheme

The following colors will be used consistently throughout the website:

Primary Color: #2c3e50 — Used for headings, navigation bar, and emphasis sections.

Background Color: #f5f5f5 — Used for the overall page background.

Accent Color: #e67e22 — Used for buttons, highlights, and calls to action.

Typography

Typography was chosen to ensure readability and a professional appearance.

  • Headings: Montserrat (modern and professional look for titles)
  • Body Text: Arial, sans-serif for clarity and simplicity

These fonts will be applied consistently across all pages of the website.

Wireframe

Mobile View

On mobile devices, the layout will be stacked vertically in this order: header, navigation, main content, and footer. This ensures readability and easy scrolling for users.

Desktop View

On larger screens, the layout will be more structured with a horizontal navigation bar, grid-based content sections, and a well-organized footer. This improves usability and visual balance.
